Students

For learners submitting drafts, sharing visual references, and leaving constructive peer critique.

1. Opening a Board & Posting Your Work

Your instructor or collaborator will typically send you a direct board link (ending in ?board=...) and any required board password.

  1. Open the shared board link in your browser and enter the board password if prompted.
  2. Click + Add Post in the top-right toolbar of the board.
  3. Choose a Post Type, give your post a clear title (such as your name and iteration/draft number), add context in Notes / Caption, and click Publish Post.
Post Type Best For Student & Collaborator Submissions
Note Design rationale, project notes, process reflections, or questions for critique.
Image Link Layout spreads, mood boards, sketches, or mockups hosted via image URL, Google Drive, or Dropbox.
Video Link Motion studies, walkthroughs, or screen recordings via YouTube, Vimeo, or direct .mp4 link.
PDF Link Multi-page decks, zines, or portfolios via a shareable PDF or Drive/Dropbox link.

2. Peer Critique, Sorting & Media Preview

  • Leave comments: Type feedback into the comment box at the bottom of any post card and press Post so classmates and collaborators can read your notes.
  • Inspect large work: Click an image to open the lightbox view, or click Preview / Open ↗ on PDF cards to inspect details.
  • Browse efficiently: Use the Sort menu in the board header to sort posts by Newest First, Oldest First, or Title (A–Z).

Educators

For faculty and board creators setting up critiques, pinning prompts, managing quotas, and syncing across devices.

1. Creating Boards, Pinning Prompts & Moderating Posts

Educators and board creators can manage up to 20 active boards at a time and control board visibility and access.

  • Create a board: Click + New Board on the dashboard, add a title and prompt/instructions in Description, choose Public or Private, and set an optional Board Password.
  • Pin briefs and rubrics: Publish a post with your assignment brief or critique guidelines and check Pin this post to the top of the board (or click 📌 Pin on an existing post) so it stays prominent in the 📌 Pinned section.
  • Moderate and organize: As the board owner, you can pin/unpin or delete (✕) posts inside your boards, or click Settings to archive completed boards and free up active board slots.
  • Share with classes or teams: Click Share inside a board to copy the ?board=... URL and distribute it via your LMS, email, or chat.

2. Cross-Device Creator Identity & Saving a PDF Copy

Board ownership and your 20-board dashboard are tied to your derived creator identity rather than a separate password database.

  1. Click your identity button in the top-right header (initially Guest Creator), enter your Display Name and Creator Passkey, and click Save Identity.
  2. When switching to another computer, tablet, or phone, sign in with the exact same Display Name and Creator Passkey to access your existing boards.
  3. Need a handout or offline reference? Click Save / Print PDF at the top of this guide to print or save a clean PDF.
Classroom Note: Students and collaborators do not need to set a creator passkey just to open a shared board link, add posts, or post comments.
